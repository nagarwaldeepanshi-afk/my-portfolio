--------------------------------------------------
CORE CONCEPT
--------------------------------------------------

The entire website is based on a visual metaphor:

YELLOW PAINT is covering the designer's digital world.

The black technological portfolio exists underneath the paint.

The user's cursor acts like a physical tool that moves through the paint.

As the cursor moves:
- yellow paint separates
- paint gets pushed away
- paint stretches
- black areas become visible
- hidden typography appears
- interface elements are revealed
- small particles move
- the underlying technology world becomes visible

The interaction should feel physical, almost like moving a wet layer of paint
with your hand.

Do NOT make the interaction look like a simple circular mouse mask.

It should feel organic, irregular and dynamic.

--------------------------------------------------
CURSOR INTERACTION
--------------------------------------------------

THIS IS THE MAIN INTERACTION OF THE WEBSITE.

When the user moves the cursor:

The yellow paint reacts physically.

The cursor should:
- push paint away
- split paint
- create irregular gaps
- stretch nearby paint
- reveal black underneath
- distort the paint boundary

The revealed black region should follow the cursor but NOT be a perfect circle.

Use an organic irregular shape.

The paint should have:
- liquid edges
- small droplets
- strings
- stretched edges
- occasional splashes

Fast cursor movement:
creates larger displacement.

Slow cursor movement:
creates a smaller reveal.

When the cursor stops:
paint slowly flows back.

This should feel similar to physically disturbing a wet painted surface.
